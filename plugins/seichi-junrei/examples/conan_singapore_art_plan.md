# Conan / Singapore — visual storyboard

This revision repairs repeated table-facing compositions. It preserves the approved investigation-notebook layout and existing cast. The trip remains a model itinerary; new character actions and composite backgrounds are imagined fan art, not additional film-location evidence.

## Budget and rhythm

- Five large illustrations: cover, three destination scenes and closing.
- Four small companion drawings, presented at four selected pauses/notes rather than every section. These use one transparent 2×2 sheet.
- Six unique files, nine distinct drawings and nine placements in the main reading flow. Story/detail dialogs reuse the appropriate artwork without requiring more unique images.
- Overview/local geography remains a map, not a generated illustration. Sources, observations and records remain live text and controls.
