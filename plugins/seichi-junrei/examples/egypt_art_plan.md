# JoJo / Egypt — whole-guide visual storyboard

## Direction before generation

Keep the approved black/gold adventure cover and every approved story illustration. Build a two-day dramatic arc: river-city observation → brothers' comic suspense → evening pause → desert-distance transition → monumental scale → prophecy close-up → playful release. This is not a new factual route or a recreation of exact scenes. Fictional/thematic connections, maps and practical conditions stay explicitly distinct.

Main flow: eight large illustrations and three companion placements, eleven placements total. Nine unique files in that flow: six approved large images, two new large images and one four-pose transparent Iggy sheet (three poses used). The three existing desert/battle story-card images remain accessible in dialogs, not counted as new main-page drawings. Keep maps, travel checks and records as live text/controls; no invented stops or times to justify an illustration.
